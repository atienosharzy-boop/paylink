Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up PayLink for your school.

Do parents need to install an app to pay? ▼
No! Parents do not need any app. They simply tap the link on WhatsApp or SMS in any phone browser, enter their phone number, and approve the M-Pesa PIN prompt directly on their phone screen.
Where does the money go when parents pay? ▼
All funds go directly to your school's existing Safaricom Paybill or Till number. PayLink does not hold your funds, keeping your school compliant with regulatory guidelines.
Can parents pay in installments (partial payments)? ▼
Yes! PayLink was specifically built for partial fee payments. A parent can pay KES 5,000 today and the remaining balance next week. The system updates the running balance in real time and prevents overpayments.
How does the M-Pesa STK push prompt work? ▼
When a parent clicks "Pay with M-Pesa", Safaricom's system sends a prompt directly to their handset asking for their M-Pesa PIN. PayLink never asks for, sees, or stores parent PINs.
How do I import our school's student roster? ▼
You can add students one by one or click "Bulk Import" on your Bursar Dashboard to paste your list of students from Excel in seconds.
